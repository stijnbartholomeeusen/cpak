Berekening

aHKA (arithmetic Hip-Knee-Ankle angle) = MPTA − LDFA

  • Varus: aHKA < −2°
  • Neutraal: −2° ≤ aHKA ≤ 2°
  • Valgus: aHKA > 2°

JLO (Joint Line Obliquity) = MPTA + LDFA

  • Apex proximaal: JLO < 177°
  • Neutraal: 177° ≤ JLO ≤ 183°
  • Apex distaal: JLO > 183°
